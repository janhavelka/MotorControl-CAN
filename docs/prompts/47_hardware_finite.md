### Concrete evidence to recheck

Recheck [wiring and DIP evidence](../audit/LICHUAN_OBJECT_FACTS.md): RJ45 pins1/2/3 are CAN_H/CAN_L/GND; SW8 ON inserts 120 ohms; SW6/SW7 select 125/250/500/1000 kbit/s. Manufacturer supply ranges conflict three ways: panel artwork and the manufacturer page say 30-110 VDC / 20-80 VAC; spec tables say 36-110 / 18-80; wiring says 24-50. Use the nameplate and identify the actual fixture before energizing. The 57C-body manual (OP86 and OP57 p5 note 4) says the motor locks on power-up; applicability to CL86-C is unconfirmed and OP86 p9 points the other way. Treat every power-up or restart as a possible torque state change, observe it independently, and record `2100`/`6041` before any command. Acquire model/firmware, raw identity, status dialect, scale and I/O applicability; don't test both disputed output addresses by trial writes. For the DO dispute use a reviewed read experiment only: read `2400:00` and `:0F`, and compare `:0C/:0D` with the DO0 default. ZH86 p19 states the EDS numbering (0C=0x2B .. 0F=0x2E) while EN86 p8/p18 and ZH86 p8 use 0B..0E; the register = sub + 0x1F formula fails at `0A` in both manuals, so this is corroboration, not a resolution. Qualification must retain exact traffic/timestamps and distinguish a source correction from a measurement on one unit.
