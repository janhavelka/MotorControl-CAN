### Concrete evidence to recheck

Use [position sequences](../audit/LICHUAN_MOTION_FACTS.md): `607A` I32 target, documented -1000000..+1000000 pulses; `6081` U32 profile speed, 0..3000 rpm; `6083/6084` U32 times, 0..2000 ms. Controlword decimal `15 -> 31` starts absolute and `15 -> 95` relative; `15 -> 63/127` sets immediate-update bit5. Bit4 is the new-target rising edge; bit6 selects relative. The documents do not establish a target FIFO, capacity or acknowledgement timing.

Falling-edge meaning in mode 1 is unspecified. Hold bit4 until the move is terminal, then write `000F` as a visible step ([DEC-8](PROMPTSET_AUDIT_34_50_2026-11-02.md#3-settled-decisions)). That is host policy, not a vendor rearm rule. Immediate update (`15->63`, `15->127`) implies a new edge during a curve, by inference, so it has its own gate. Stops from 31 or 95 are not documented arrows; use the DEC-8 table in [prompt 31](31_stop_actions.md) and carry `predecessorDocumented=false`.

The documented range is not the admission range. Reject `6083/6084`=0 under Q11, and `6081`=0 for motion, before TX until zero semantics are evidenced. `preparePositionParameters` stays pure arithmetic; this rejection belongs to move preparation.

All sources define bit15 as position-mode in-place, listed only in the position-mode table; its meaning in modes 3 and 6 is undocumented. Its deassert behavior, `250B/250C` dependence and soft-limit stop behavior are unqualified. Bit12 is EN86-only; without it, acceptance needs another evidenced signal or the move stays gated. EN86 bit10 is zero speed, not target reached. Completion bit15 must be fresh for this operation. The status dialect dispute covers the alarm and motion bits and stays a gate.

Measured, for one power cycle on the two S2 bench specimens only ([record](../reports/2026-11-02_pdo_motion_plan.md)): `6061=0`, `2100=1`, `607A=5000`, `60FF=120`. The two targets equal the EDS defaults, so these reads do not show whether a target was commanded. They establish no mode, enable or motion fact for other firmware.

Evidence route: prompt 29 conditional experiment or manufacturer clarification; do not wait for prompt 47.

## Implement

1. Implement `prepareMoveAbsolute`, `prepareMoveRelative`, `nextMove` and `advanceMove` using `profiles/lichuan_c/Position.h`, common `MoveOperation.h` and existing owner work/events. The concrete terminal context is the result; do not add `getMoveResult`.
2. Capture immutable intent, effective target, quantization error, scale/origin/limits and binding/configuration/dialect generations. Validate native target/rate/ramp widths and allowed ranges without clamping, truncation or ESS tested limits. Relative actual/commanded/queued bases require explicit evidenced semantics. `6081` is rpm, and `preparePositionParameters` already takes native rpm. Never write `effectiveNative` to an rpm object until the basis is rpm. Quantize once and report error in rpm. Define `native` for CAN velocity in the grammar (A-01).
3. Implement the reviewed finite phases directly. Preconditions: mode 1 observed and drive explicitly enabled (prompt 30; no implicit enable). Phases in documented order: confirm mode and enabled evidence, write parameters, write the bit4 edge, confirm the protocol reply, then gather new acceptance and completion evidence. Hold `001F` or `005F` through those phases; write `000F` only after the move is terminal. After a stop or an unknown trigger that `000F` is not automatic: it would be a new enable-word write, so it needs its own explicit admission and fresh evidence (project rule from "no implicit enabling"; the audit's DEC-8 does not separate this case). Successful SDO or an old target-reached bit cannot finish a new move. Keep submitted bytes/objects, readback and timing sufficient to explain partial setup or an unknown trigger.
   - State the full transition. Enabling in mode 3 or 6 (documented only from a non-enabled start; mode 3 order conflicts, Q03) and changing `6060` while enabled (no documented sequence, Q11) are separate gated actions. Reject before TX if the observed mode differs and the drive is enabled. Enable in mode 6 is an explicit prompt-30-style action, not a homing side effect. The manuals document no way from homing to a position move on an enabled drive (D-02).
   - A move's own parameter writes (`607A`, `6081`, `6083`, `6084`) are phases of the move. They do not go through `prepareDriverSettings` and do not advance the configuration generation. They do retire retained `MOTION_PARAMETERS` observations, so a later settings call needs a fresh read; without this, settings reports success with no TX while the drive holds the move's value. An explicit settings call still advances the generation (A-02).
4. Integrate deadline, cancellation, real stop interruption, fault/reboot and stale/missing feedback with retained outcomes. A request prepared under generation A cannot execute under B. Recovery never resumes an interrupted move.
   - A running move can be stopped (C-16). The held trigger word is an admitted predecessor in prompt 31's DEC-8 table. Allowing it is a project decision, not vendor evidence. Do not rearm to `000F` mid-curve to regain a predecessor. A stop waits for an in-flight poll to settle.
   - After an unknown trigger the route is unresolved. Ordinary work and new motion stay refused, and only prompt 31's best-effort `000B` can be sent (DEC-2). Its reply never qualifies and it never settles the trigger's uncertainty. The route reopens only through prompt 31's explicit drain marker (DEC-3), which settles the channel only: the trigger stays uncertain, `000F` history stays consumed, new motion needs a new reservation and fresh prerequisites, and nothing replays or re-enables. An acknowledged trigger with missing completion leaves the route resolved, and the move context still inhibits new motion. A matched abort of a trigger leaves the route open.
   - Tell a reply lost after trigger from an exhausted budget. Use the distinct service-timeout outcome that W8 adds for queue and response budgets, plus `transmissionPossible` for a reply lost after trigger, and do not copy the current mapping of SDO TIMEOUT to DEADLINE into `MoveOutcome` (A-08).
   - Apply one processing-time cutoff: `now >= deadlineUs` expires, even if the frame was received earlier ([CONVENTIONS section 3](CONVENTIONS.md#3-motion-typed-reads-and-terminal-evidence)).
5. Make configuration/profile snapshot reuse explicit and invalidate it on contradictory observations. Writing `6083/6084` in either mode invalidates the other mode's snapshot. Move preparation rejects zero effective displacement with no writes; pure arithmetic may report it. Use `validatePreparedTarget` at admission. An absolute target with no witness keeps unknown displacement and claims no position or standstill. If a finite onboard trajectory requires a documented refresh lease, service that lease without replaying a relative trigger or restarting the trajectory.
6. Keep controller-observed completion separate from independent physical movement/standstill evidence. Missing firmware facts block affected motion capability, not unrelated unit arithmetic or owner tests.
7. This prompt owns the `607A` read row: a typed read of the raw I32 stored value, which is not evidence that the drive accepted a target. `607A` is not on the DEC-1 accepted list, so its read effect stays gated by Q20.

## Verify and exit

Test positive/negative absolute and relative boundaries, native overflow, exact quantization, all supported bases, zero displacement, snapshot reuse/mismatch, mode/setup failure, trigger history, stale acceptance/completion and completion without matching reference. Interrupt at every phase with stop, fault, cancellation, reboot and deadline; test timeout after trigger followed by attempted new motion, and a best-effort stop after a lost trigger reply that stays unqualified with an identical late reply. Test that bit4 stays held until a terminal and that `000F` then appears as its own step, that a mid-curve stop comes from the held trigger word with no `000F` preamble, that zero `6081`, `6083` and `6084` reject before TX, that a mode mismatch on an enabled drive rejects before TX, and that a move's parameter writes keep its prepared target valid while a later settings call needs a fresh read.

Run through the real fake owner path and compare common intent vectors to RS without copying its device frames. Comparison vectors set every field explicitly; do not rely on defaults, because they differ: `PositionRequest.relative` is true in RS and false in CAN, `VelocityRequest.unit` is turns/minute in RS and steps/second in CAN, and the quantization allowance is `double` in RS and `Rational` in CAN. Exit with complete confirmed absolute/relative behavior and native examples independent of CLI. Do not mark unresolved dialect behavior implemented merely because simulated events pass. Run position/axis/action/owner regressions and Doxygen.
