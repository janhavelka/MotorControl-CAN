### Concrete evidence to recheck

Use the [method-by-method homing table](../audit/LICHUAN_MOTION_FACTS.md). `6098` methods 17/18 use negative/positive limit; 24/29 use origin in positive/negative search directions. Method 29 finishes on leaving the switch during positive creep, not simply the reverse of method 24. `6099:01/:02` are U32 search/creep RPM (0..3000); `609A` is U32 ramp ms (0..2000); `607C` is I32 offset (-1000000..+1000000). Mode 6 and controlword bit4 rising/falling edges start/interrupt. Numeric backoff distance and search timeout are unspecified. Reject the wrong two-byte writes to `6098` (one byte), `6099:01/:02` and `609A` (four bytes), and the 5000 ms ramp, in the example at `docs/reference/public_code/srt/SRT_OpenCan.cpp:146-152`; 5000 ms exceeds the documented 0..2000 range. Add wrong-width and out-of-range vectors. Controlword itself remains two bytes. The documented ranges are not the admission range: reject `609A`=0 under Q11 and `6099:01/:02`=0 for motion before TX until zero semantics are evidenced. `607C` is in the EN86 p13 table but not in p15's write list; write order, units, sign and effect on the final coordinate are unqualified (Q14, Q21). `6099:02` is named "creep time (rpm)" in EN86/ZH86 p13 but is creep velocity in the steps (editorial note only). Rates share the A-01 rpm basis: never write `effectiveNative` to an rpm object unless the prepared `rateBasis` is `MOTOR_TURNS`, its `rateTime` is `MINUTE` and its representation is integer. `nativeVelocityTime` defaults to `SECOND` and the basis to `COMMAND_INCREMENTS`, and both give in-range wrong values: 1 load turn/min at 3,200 increments per turn and gear 5 gives 267. A negative test must pin that such a target is refused before any write. Reject a nonzero request that rounds to zero.

Evidence route: prompt 29 conditional experiment or manufacturer clarification; do not wait for prompt 47. Processing-time cutoff follows [CONVENTIONS](CONVENTIONS.md#3-motion-typed-reads-and-terminal-evidence): `now >= deadlineUs` expires, even if the frame was received earlier.

3. State the full transition. Validate the whole candidate before writes, including ranges, signedness, state, binding/configuration generation and physical prerequisite evidence where needed. Starting on an active switch and meeting a limit during an origin search are undocumented, so they are gated. `60FD` has no documented bit layout; do not substitute it for `2101`. Capture raw `2200` in the configuration generation; its effect on homing direction is undocumented, so method 17 could run away from its switch if it applies. 4. Hold bit 4 = 1 (host word `0x001F`, inferred from mode 1's `15->31`; the manuals give only bit 4 edges) until a terminal result. Write bit 4 = 0 only as the deliberate interrupt or after completion; do not share the position rearm path, because EN86 p15 and ZH86 p15 give homing bit 4 "1->0: Stopping homing" and a position-style 31-then-15 rearm would interrupt the home. Host origin, device counter clear and a successful home reference remain different facts.
