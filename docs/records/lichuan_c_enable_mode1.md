## Reading the citations

- `docs/bench/<folder>/rx.txt:N` is line N of a loose capture. `docs/bench/<campaign>/packed/<series>.tar.xz!<member>:N`
  is line N of the member file inside that xz tar archive; read it without extracting to the tree, for example
  `tar -xOf <archive> <member> | sed -n Np`. Lines count from 1; every capture line is ASCII.
- A ledger cell `<ledger>.jsonl:A,B` gives the rows of the result ledger whose `dir` or `output` field names the
  capture's folder (normally a `send` and a `result` row). `none` means no ledger row names it.
- A number before a colon in a cell is the line of the named capture that holds the cited values.
- The bench line formats are `examples/s2_bench/BenchRoute.cpp` (`TRIAL_PLAN`, `TRIAL_EVIDENCE`,
  `TRIAL_VELOCITY_SAMPLE` and the others). State and outcome numbers are `ActionState` (2 `SUCCEEDED`, 3 `FAILED`)
  and `ActionOutcome` (1 `OBSERVED`, 5 `CANCELLED`, 6 `DEADLINE`, 8 `OBSERVATION_SCOPE`) of
  `include/MotorControlCAN/ActionOperation.h`.
- The tables were generated by a read-only scan of every `rx.txt` under `docs/bench`, loose and packed, and of every
  `*.jsonl` ledger there. Raw evidence was not edited.
