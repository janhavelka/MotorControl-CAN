The protocol corrections were checked against [CiA's SDO description](https://www.can-cia.org/can-knowledge/sdo-protocol), [CANopenNode's client API](https://canopennode.github.io/CANopenNode/group__CO__SDOclient.html) and [client implementation](https://github.com/CANopenNode/CANopenNode/blob/master/301/CO_SDOclient.c). [CiA's NMT description](https://www.can-cia.org/can-knowledge/network-management) establishes the service distinction: SDO is available in pre-operational and operational, PDO in operational, and stopped devices retain NMT/error-control behavior. The prompt's treatment of heartbeat-disabled service evidence is a design policy built on those rules, not a claim that an SDO reply identifies the exact NMT state. The Lichuan EDS's `0x1017` default is zero; defaults are evidence to review, never a startup write recipe.

## Design retained and bounded

Keep one library for stepper and servo drives, with Lichuan C over Classical CANopen first. The N5/EPOS4 contrasts justify common intent, units, axis identity and evidence boundaries; they do not justify installing placeholder profiles. Other CAN protocols remain separate future backends.

Keep simple C++11 free functions, concrete caller-owned contexts, one application CAN receiver, explicit time and fixed storage. Exact arithmetic, independent outcome/effect evidence and channel correlation rules solve real motor-control problems. Replacing these with floating-point shortcuts, booleans or automatic retry would lose required meaning. Conversely, no runtime plugin framework, universal transaction engine, generic event bus or full fake vendor stack is needed. Reuse the same small contrast fixtures and the chosen service backend.

The original manufacturer conflicts remain unresolved: status/motion/alarm bit interpretations, velocity-start ordering/signedness and model/firmware applicability. The millisecond units for `0x6083/0x6084/0x609A`, `0x2300` reset/save values and `0x2302` edge behavior remain source-specific facts to review. Generic CiA 402 assumptions cannot settle a Lichuan contradiction. These prompts keep affected operations gated for better evidence; no hardware session was run.
