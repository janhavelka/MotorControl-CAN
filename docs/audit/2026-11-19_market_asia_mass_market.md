# Asian mass-market CAN drives: market and documentation survey

Research date 2026-11-19. Market and documentation research only: no product is implemented, supported or hardware-tested by this note. It extends the [CANopen survey](CANOPEN_DRIVE_SURVEY.md) (its Delta A2-M row) and [other CAN protocols](OTHER_CAN_PROTOCOLS.md) (CubeMars/MyActuator were only named there), within [the multidrive decision](../multidrive_decision.md).

Evidence marks: **(text)** read in the original PDF text by this survey; **(page)** read on a vendor or retailer web page; **(unverified)** from a search summary or third-party copy only. PDFs were read through the fetch tool's session cache, not stored in the repository. Prices are retail snapshots, not procurement advice. EtherCAT-only, pulse-only and RS485-only variants are excluded.

## Ranked promptset recommendation

1. **MKS SERVO42D/57D CAN: own promptset with a new proprietary backend.** It is the most common and cheapest product here, has a current public manual pinned to firmware with a version history, and existing third-party code to compare against. It exercises 11-bit addressing, checksums, broadcast/group commands without replies, suppressible replies, a drive-reported "complete" status and a host-silence stop. Decide whether it precedes ODrive.
2. **Applied Motion / MOONS' STF-C and SSDC-C: own promptset on the existing CANopen layer.** It is the best-documented CANopen product here, with public hardware manuals from 2024-2025, and it covers open-loop, closed-loop stepper and servo classes. First obtain the 2021 CANopen manual and EDS by hand.
3. **Kinco FD1X4S-CA / iSMK: own CANopen promptset** for the AGV low-voltage servo class. It has vendor mode values, interpolation and a documented EDS. Get the FD-specific CANopen manual first.
4. **Delta ASDA-B3-M (and A3-M):** an industrial AC servo profile. Make B3-M the exact target in place of the older A2-M once the original EN manual and EDS are downloadable.
5. **Leadshine EM556-CAN / ELD2-CAN / iSV2-CAN:** only after the DM-CAN technical manual, the EDS and redistribution terms are obtained.
6. **ZLTECH ZLAC8015D:** after its CANopen object manual is obtained. It is a good dual-axis contrast.

Defer INVT SV-DA200 (good guide, unclear CAN-variant market), Inovance SV660C and JMC iHSS57-RC (documentation not obtained). Damiao and CubeMars need new backends, and their impedance-control-first actuators fit a separate robotics-actuator survey better than this positioning-drive segment.

No sibling repository was inspected beyond the [RS promptset shape](../../../MotorControl-RS/docs/prompts/lichuan_lc57bk_rs485/README.md). No vendor was contacted and no hardware was used.
