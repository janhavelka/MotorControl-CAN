## Trial 1009: velocity, node 1

[Record](../bench/20261109T1446Z-p3536-velocity-node1-1009/). The library's attended experiment (`gate=2 start=0 stop=0`), 6 rpm, ramps 200 ms, duration 12000 ms, HALT, layout ZH86.

Observed, in order (S2 clock):

1. Downloads `60FF=6`, `6083=200`, `6084=200`, then words 6, 7 and 15, each acknowledged. The cycle after word 6 read `6041=8031`, `2100=0011`; after word 7 `8033`, `0001`; after word 15 again `8033`, `0001`, `6064=0`, `606C=0`: its `6041` read completed 28 ms after word 15's acknowledgement, and the cycle spanned +4 to +49 ms.
2. The library ended the start as unconfirmed: `TRIAL_VELOCITY state=3 phase=5 outcome=8 int=4 completion=0 stop=0 sexec=0 uncertain=1 needs=1 fenced=1 running=0 code=1 detail=3`. Its stop was requested at that instant (`stopAt=502842967`), but no stop was started (`TRIAL_STOP own=0`) and nothing more was sent. The record ended at once.
3. [1019](../bench/20261109T1446Z-p3536-p1-node1-1019/), a read-only readiness route sent because node 1 might be turning, found it running at 539.55 s: `6041=C437`, `2100=0004`, `606C=5`, `6064` 3595 and then 3604.
4. [1020](../bench/20261109T1447Z-p3536-stop-quick-node1-1020/), an explicit quick stop, was refused by the runner before anything was sent, because the lead had not released 1019's record. About 20 s were lost.
5. [1021](../bench/20261109T1447Z-p3536-stop-quick-node1-1021/), the same quick stop, was admitted at 570.91 s. Its first read got no reply and no acknowledgement (`tec1=128`): by then the user had opened the cutoff. No `000B` was sent.
6. The user reported "Turned, I cut power" and "Both drives off", and, asked afterwards, "I believe it was clockwise, but not 100% sure", viewed facing the shaft end.

Inferred, not measured: if `6064` counts 1000 per motor turn (`2201=1000`) and the motor ran at a constant 6 rpm, 3595 counts at 539.55 s put the start about 0.8 s after word 15. In mode 1, word 15 reported `8437` in the first cycle (1004 and earlier enables); in mode 3 the operation-enabled report came later than one cycle. This is one observation on one firmware.

Correction, made the same day after the host reproduction: an earlier version of this paragraph said that in mode 1 word 15 reported `8437` in the first cycle. It does not. In all 13 retained records that sent word 15 (12 mode 1 enables on both nodes, 2026-11-03 to 2026-11-09, including 1004, and 1009), the first cycle after the acknowledgement read `6041=8033`, its `6041` read 23 to 28 ms after the acknowledgement; in mode 1 the next cycle, 850 to 880 ms later, read `8437` (1004: 876 ms). The enable action re-reads until it sees `8437`, so it passed; `TRIAL_ENABLE_STEP` prints the accepted cycle, not the first. In mode 3 the time to the report is unmeasured: nothing was read between +49 ms and 37 s.

Defect (backlog S16): after an enabling word may have left (`needs=1`), the library requested its HALT, but `AxisSession::requestStop` refused it. Three independent gates refused it: the fresh report after the velocity command was idle in mode 3; `validateStop` needs low bits 111 and `2100` running in mode 3; and a HALT has no best-effort path. The session then ended the run INVALIDATED with nothing sent. A literal host replay of this record (`velocity_owner`, `Replay1009`) reproduces the record field for field and fails on the code that ran. The fix sends the run's own stop as soon as its acknowledged `000F` is still the latest word, whatever the drive reports, and keeps the fence (see the CHANGELOG).

## End state

Both drives unpowered by the cutoff at about 14:47Z. The S2 was then EN-reset ([record](../bench/20261109T1449Z-p3536-en-reset/)), because its controller held 1021's unacknowledged frame retrying on the unpowered bus; it booted silent (`active=0`), sending nothing. Nothing else was sent.
