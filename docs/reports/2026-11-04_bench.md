## What the drives showed

**`606C` is in rpm** on this configuration: it read 30 at 30 rpm, while `6064` advanced about 500 pulses/s, and 1 at 2 rpm (packet 15's open unit).

**The 251-then-250 count of 2026-11-03** did not recur: the same move on the same node completed at 250 and held 250 for 8 s, sampled every 0.5 to 0.7 s. One overshoot of one pulse is still possible; a dither inside the in-place window was not seen.

**`000B` during a move releases the motor and latches the running bit.** The first soak reads, 4 minutes later, showed node 2 at `6064=1187`, about 10 pulses past the stop's last move sample, still `2100=0014` and `6041=4050`, with `606C=0`. So `2100` bit 2 reads set on a released, still motor while its move target stays unreached. "Not running" cannot end such a stop.

**A stationary `010F` from `000F`** changes no reported bit: the drive stays enabled and in place.

**Word 7 pulls a displaced shaft back** (packet 15). Released after its coasted quick stop, node 2 read 1187; at word 7 it energized, swung to 1194 and settled at 1180, near where its trajectory was when `000B` arrived. So word 7 restores the drive's last commanded position: it neither resets the counter nor adopts the actual position. That also explains trial 105 of 2026-11-03 (`6064` from −545 to 0 at word 7 after a hand check): a pull-back of about half a turn. **Never move a released shaft by hand before an enable**, and expect motion at word 7 whenever the shaft was displaced. One specimen, a 7-pulse displacement.

**`000B` on a released drive does nothing** (packet 16 step 3): the best-effort path's word is harmless in that state on this firmware.

**Relative moves add to the actual position** (packet 21): from 104 with last target 600, +200 ended at 304. So `RelativeBase::ACTUAL` is measured on node 1, and the library's own relative move then reached its computed endpoint. One specimen, one firmware, a positive displacement.

**Clearing a halt does not resume it**: `000F` after a moving `010F` left the drive at 104, 496 pulses short of its target, and a later relative move started from there.

**The counter zero zeroes `6064` on an energized drive** with no reported motion. Whether an internal command counter changed with it is not shown.

**DI writes are volatile**: the `2400:01` polarity write was gone after a power cycle (one cycle, one field, no save sent). The inverted polarity showed in `2101` bit 3 but not in `60FD`.

**Device facts against the EDS.** `60FE:00` and `607D:00`, the entry counts of two arrays, are two-byte replies of 0 on both specimens, where the EDS declares U8, so the strict typed `IO_OUTPUT_COMMAND` (60FE) and `SOFT_LIMITS` groups cannot complete (`INPUTS`, 60FD, read normally); the value read is 0, which may mean no subentries. The output configuration reads `2400:0C=0`, `:0D=1`, which packet 13 P1 reads as the EDS layout without identifying a function. The filter words `2401..2407` read 2, 1, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0: `2401` equals OP86's 2, and none equals its EDS default (50, 40, 100, 4000, 10, 2, 16). The tuning image is identical on both specimens; `2502=8400` does not open packet 14 T1, which also needs a phase-current probe.
