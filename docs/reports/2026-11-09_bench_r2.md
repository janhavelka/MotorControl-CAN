# Bench round, 2026-11-09 11:16Z to 11:26Z: prompts 35 to 38 on the S2, unattended

First hardware run of the bench integration of prompts 35 to 38 (`757f1cd`, [upload](../bench/20261109T111649Z-s2-upload/), ELF `f35c3c01…`, PONG `routes=6 pdo=4`). The user was absent and wrote: "I will not be rpesent - do all the tests yourself, if you move motors read how they moved, dont ask me." That statement is the `--unattended-waiver` of every attended route here; [ledger](../bench/20261109-r2-campaign.jsonl). Nothing was enabled and nothing moved.

## Start state: the drives were not power-cycled

The user was asked to power-cycle both drives and reported node 1 HOLDING and node 2 FREE. The first reads show neither power-up state (`6041=8037`, `6061=0`, `6064=0`), but the campaign's end state: node 1 enabled (`6041=0437`, `6061=1`) at `6064=158`, where the campaign left it at 146; node 2 released (`8050`, `2100=0011`, `6061=1`) at -49, where it was released at 304 ([901, 902](../bench/20261109T1118Z-r2-p1-node1-901/)). The reported shaft states match those: node 1 holding because it is enabled, node 2 free because it is released. The position changes are consistent with the shafts turned by hand; that is an inference, the drive reports only the counts. With no power cycle, no enable was attempted: node 1 has no host history in the S2's boot and no reviewed release transition, and node 2 has had motion writes since its last power-up. The relayed-shaft option added for this round (`s2_bench.py --relayed-power-up-shaft`) was therefore not used.

## Integration findings

- **Route sessions block `K` and `H` for the rest of a boot.** The bench keeps each node's `AxisSession` open between routes, and each holds its axis's owner reservation; `K` and `H` begin a sequence of their own and are refused `busy` (11:19Z, after routes 901 and 902). Workaround used: an EN reset of the S2 through the ESP-Prog-2, then `K` and `H` before any route.
- **`K` and `H` grant draws block the fixed-grant routes for the rest of a boot.** After `K`, the homing route's `2400` read was refused `why=grant` after its identity reads (903 to 906, zero downloads). Workaround used: another EN reset, homing first.
- Both MCU resets were EN pulses through RTS with DTR deasserted (the hard reset esptool performs after an upload), recorded in [`r2-en-reset`](../bench/20261109T1120Z-r2-en-reset/) and [`r2c-en-reset`](../bench/20261109T1125Z-r2c-en-reset/), each with the script that ran.

## Not tested, and why

- Packet 8's remaining case (a quick stop interrupting a moving `005F`), node 2's production relative move with its own record 830, and every enable: the drives were not power-cycled (above).
- Velocity motion: not run unattended. Its only bound is its own stop: a lost stop leaves the motor turning with nobody at the cutoff, unlike a position move bounded by its target. Its route also needs `VelocityEvidence` start and stop qualification that no record establishes (Q03, Q12); the route takes them from the host line as an experiment scope. A library experiment gate (as the enable has) would be the honest form.
- The homing CLOSED-gate refusal and any homing motion: no origin or limit switch is known to be wired, and the gate stays closed.
