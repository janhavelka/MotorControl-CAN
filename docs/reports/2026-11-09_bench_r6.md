## S16: the run's own stop after an unconfirmed final enable

Downloads `60FF=6`, `6083=200`, `6084=200`, then words 6 (next cycle `8031`/`0011`), 7 (`8033`/`0001`) and 15, sent at 139.587 s and acknowledged; the next cycle's `6041` read, 28 ms after the acknowledgement, again `8033`/`0001`, `6064=0`, `606C=0`. No fallback stop was needed, and the runner raised no alert.
3. Two read-only routes 9 s and 16 s later ([1309](../bench/20261109T1602Z-s16-p1-node1-1309/), [1310](../bench/20261109T1602Z-s16-p1-node1-1310/)) found node 1 operation enabled, halted, stationary: `6041=9437`, `2100=0001`, `6061=3`, `6064=0`, `606C=0`.

Not observed: the shaft. The user answered "Didn't watch", so motion is judged from the drive's reports only (`6064` 0 in every read). Inferred, not measured: the drive completed the enable after the HALT had arrived, and the HALT bit kept it at zero speed.

End state: node 1 enabled in mode 3 with `60FF=6`, held by the HALT bit; the user switched both drives off at about 16:11Z. Nothing else was sent. No EMCY, `1001=00`, `603F=0000` throughout.

## What this establishes, and what not

- S16's fix works for the 1009 shape on this firmware: after an acknowledged `000F` with no enable report, the HALT leaves within one cycle and the drive stays at standstill by its reports. One run, one specimen, shaft not watched.
- A velocity run on this firmware cannot start until S17 is resolved: the first cycle after word 15 never shows the enable. S17 needs a mode 3 measurement of the time to the enable report.
- Not run: node 2, any quick stop in mode 3, the fallback quick stop on hardware (it was not needed).
