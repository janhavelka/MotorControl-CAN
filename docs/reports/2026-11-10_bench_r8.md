Observed, one firmware, two specimens, drive reports only:

- **Start:** the enable report came in the same window cycle on every run, at every rate (6, 30 and 60 rpm, rounds 7 to 9): two `8033` cycles, then operation enabled at +0.728 s.
- **Rate:** `6064` rose 0.992 times the commanded rate in counts at 1000 counts per turn, at 6, 30 and 60 rpm alike: 99.2, 496.0 and 992.0 counts/s. The constant ratio says the shortfall is systematic, not noise; its cause (drive speed, counts per turn, or the S2 clock) is not established. The heartbeat period measured on the S2 clock was 1.0008 s against 1000 ms, too small to explain 0.8%.
- **HALT in mode 3:** at the due time, `010F`; the drive reported running 28 ms later and halted in place 4.04 s later, still enabled (`6041=9437`, `2100=0001`, `6061=3`). Travel after the due time: 60 counts at 30 rpm and 121 counts at 60 rpm, about a 230 ms deceleration from the extrapolated due-time position (inferred; the 200 ms ramp would give 50 and 99).
- **Quick stop in mode 3 (first measurement):** at the due time, `000B`; 28 ms later the drive already reported itself released with running latched (`2100=0014`, `6041=C050`), and afterwards released at standstill with in place (`2100=0015`). Travel after the due time: 8 counts at 30 rpm, 13 counts at 60 rpm, which is under about 10 ms of motion (inferred). The library's mode 3 stop scope does not accept a released report as its settlement, so these runs end `OBSERVATION_SCOPE`, uncertain and fenced, by design.

Not observed: the shafts (no attendant observation was asked for), torque after the quick stop, the deceleration curve within the 4 s, reverse and zero targets. Node 1 ended released, node 2 enabled and halted in mode 3; the user then switched both drives off.
