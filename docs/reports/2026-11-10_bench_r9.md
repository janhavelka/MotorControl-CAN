## Observed, one firmware, two specimens, drive reports only

- **A power-cycle-free loop exists:** velocity runs ending with a quick stop leave the drive released at standstill with running latched (`0015`/`C050`), from which the next run is admitted and succeeds (cycles 5, 7, 8, 9: four in a row, both nodes, 6 to 60 rpm).
- **Word 7 in mode 3:** clears the latched running bit (cycle 3), and re-energizing moves the rotor to the drive's internal command before any observation: +12, +5, +5, +1, +10 and −6 counts in cycles 3, 4, 5, 7, 8 and 9; 0 after a power-up (rounds 7 to 9). After each release the rotor relaxes back (8424 to 8419 on node 2).
- **Quick stop from the halted state** (`9437`/`0001` after a HALT, sent as best effort after an MCU restart) released the drive to `8050`/`0011`, one count of travel.
- **Start window:** the enable report came in the same window cycle in every run (+0.728 s after word 15), now on 15 runs from rounds 7 to 9 and this chain.
- **Rate:** 0.992 times the command at 6, 15, 30, 45 and 60 rpm: 99.3, 248.1, 496.0, 744.2 and 992.1 counts/s.

Not observed: the shafts, torque after the quick stops, whether the word 7 pull has a bound. End state: both drives powered, released at standstill with running latched (node 1 at 6761, node 2 at 25235), nothing more sent.
