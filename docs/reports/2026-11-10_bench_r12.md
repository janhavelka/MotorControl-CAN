Observed, one firmware, two specimens: 23 completed arrivals over about 125 motor turns, each exactly on its target in counts, absolute and relative alike, with no drift between cycles; every `move` `SUCCEEDED` and every `position` reported `9437`/`0001` at its target. The re-enables (2302, 2322, 2342, 2402) each passed with re-energize pulls of −1, −4, 0 and 0 counts, and the stationary quick stops released the drives without a latch. Counts are the drive's own report; no independent angle measurement was made (Q04).

Lead errors in series 2340: the first absolute move started at −5000, where node 1's series 2300 had ended, so it covered 5500 counts at 6 rpm, about 55 s, while the window was sized for 1000 counts; the route's 14 cycles ended with the drive still moving (2343: `5037`, −3718, `606C=5`), and the script's rearm (2344) was refused by the firmware before any write (`failure=8 why=running`) but counted against the campaign's rearm cap. The move completed by itself at +500 (read 2350), and the series continued in the same boot one cycle short. While moving at a commanded 6 rpm, `606C` read 5, 6 and 7 in different cycles. The script now sizes each window from the actual distance and stops if a move is not reported complete.

End state: node 1 released at +500, node 2 released at −2500, both `0011`/`8050` in mode 1; nothing more sent.
